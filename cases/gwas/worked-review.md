Corrected conclusion: The synthetic table gives a pooled carrier odds ratio of approximately 4.52 but a ratio of 1 within each stratum. Unequal stratum composition explains this contrast. These descriptive calculations do not establish genome-wide significance, causality, or the responsible gene.

For a real analysis, inspect sample and variant QC, relatedness, population structure and covariate adjustment, effect estimates and uncertainty, multiple-testing procedures, and replication. Do not infer that a fixed number of principal components automatically resolves confounding.
