# ABE-007 independent assignment

Original synthetic CDI teaching scenario. All measurements and labels are invented. This assignment requires arithmetic and evidence review; no R script is supplied so that you can choose and document your own verification approach. A calculator or spreadsheet is sufficient.

## Question
Does the supplied evidence support a claim that a dietary change increased the absolute abundance of Feature A and improved health?

## Study record
Three distinct participants were sampled before and after a dietary change. No untreated comparison group, randomization, absolute microbial-load measurement, or clinical outcome is supplied. Feature A and Other represent the entire retained feature table for this exercise. Sample identity and visits in the table are authoritative within the teaching scenario. There are no missing entries. Pre-filter data, laboratory QC, and contamination controls are not supplied.

| Sample | Participant | Visit | Feature A reads | Other reads |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| P1_B | P1 | before | 20 | 80 |
| P1_A | P1 | after | 40 | 160 |
| P2_B | P2 | before | 30 | 70 |
| P2_A | P2 | after | 60 | 140 |
| P3_B | P3 | before | 40 | 60 |
| P3_A | P3 | after | 80 | 120 |

## Simulated AI response
1. All three participants have paired before and after samples.
2. Feature A read count doubled in every participant.
3. Therefore, Feature A relative abundance increased by 100% in every participant.
4. The six independent participants provide evidence that the diet doubled the absolute abundance of Feature A.
5. The result is statistically significant and demonstrates improved gut health.
6. All samples passed contamination checks because there are no missing values.

## Required work
Write a complete evaluation of all six statements. Split mixed claims as necessary. Verify at least two quantitative claims, retain supported statements, and identify the most consequential unsupported conclusion. Explain what the table can and cannot establish.

Use the report and log templates. Select your verification tool and retain formulas, commands, or calculation notes. Submit the initial review before consulting self-review.md. Record any subsequent corrections separately.
